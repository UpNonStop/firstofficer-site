Pour the coffee · the big one is a skip

The year’s biggest door ends Thursday, July 30, at 11:59 PM ET.
Most of you should walk past it.

Every Saturday, First Officer reads the week in loyalty programs and hands you what matters. This week the read is harder to say out loud: the largest transfer bonus of the year ends Thursday, July 30, at 11:59 PM ET, and for most wallets the right move is to let it go. Here is the arithmetic behind that.

What it means for your wallet
Stronger. A quiet 20% door: Bank C to Airline E at 1:1.2 through Saturday, August 22. Into a currency we value near 1.5 cents, that returns about 1.8 cents per point, nearly double what the headline bonus returns.
Weaker. The 100% Hotel D bonus has a floor problem. Hotel D sells its own points near 0.5 cents, so doubling into them lands near 1.0 cent per Bank A point. The biggest number on the board is the smallest return on it.
Window. The 100% window ends Thursday, July 30, at 11:59 PM ET, then reopens Friday, July 31, at 70% through Monday, August 31. The door narrows. Panic transfers are the trap.
