Field note 02 · The floor most people settle for

The note, in four points.

01Learn

A statement credit is the floor

50,000 points as a statement credit come to about $500. Moved to a travel partner for a trip you wanted anyway, they can be worth more, est.

02Learn

Simple is a design choice

Cash back keeps the transfer partners out of sight. If you never learn that points can move to airline and hotel programs, you keep taking the floor.

03Learn

The gap adds up

A business spending $60,000 a year at 2% gets about $1,200. Routed and redeemed with a plan, the same spend can be worth more in travel, est. Sometimes it is not, and the desk says so.

04Learn

Cash back still has a place

If you will never travel or your spend is small, a flat 2% card is a fine answer. That is why the desk compares against it every month.
