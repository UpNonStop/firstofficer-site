08 · Where the money hides

Three leaks.
Three fixes.

Most businesses lose value in the same three places, and none of the fixes ask you to spend differently.Three places value leaks. None of the fixes change what you buy.

Leak 01 · Earn

Your spend codes wrong.

Wholesale vendors, insurance, fuel and freight often earn 1x. Ads, software and fuel can earn more on the right card.

✓ The fix: move the vendor to the right card. What you buy stays the same.

Leak 02 · Burn

Your points get spent cheaply.

A plain cash-out prices a point at about a cent. A well-planned transfer can be worth more, est.

✓ The fix: the desk plans where points go, and books only after you approve.

Leak 03 · Return

Value quietly runs out.

Credits reset, certificates lapse and balances can expire. None of it announces itself.

✓ The fix: the desk watches the clocks and flags each one in time.
