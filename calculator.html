    How we got this number

    The math, in plain words.
    Four steps, no magic.

    The same four steps run on your Found Money Call, from your real statements. Every figure here is an estimate.

    01Earn

    Each line goes to its best card

    Ads and software to the 4x card, internet and phone to 3x, the rest to 2x. That is your desk's points a month, next to what your cards earn today.

    02Count

    Only 85% of the new points count

    Some points always go unused, so 15% stays out of every estimate. Your points today are not counted at all, only the extra.

    03Value

    Valued at how you will use them

    A cash out is the floor, about 1¢ a point. Planned trips run higher. The answer is the middle of that range, and the range is shown.

    04Fee

    Then the $550 comes off

    What your desk finds, against $550 a month, flat. Return is the one divided by the other. Under ~$25K a month on cards, it says not a fit yet.

    Every assumption, line by line
    Basic
    One spend figure at a blended rate: about 1.2 points per $1 today and about 3.4 with your desk. That is a service business like the insurance agency mix, so Basic at $35K matches it.
    Advanced
    Your spend by line, at the rate shown for each line, today and with your desk. "Your cards today" can swap your rates for one flat card.
    Cents a point
    Mixed 1.0¢ to 1.6¢, cash out 1.0¢, economy 1.0¢ to 1.4¢, top hotels 1.2¢ to 2.0¢, business class 1.5¢ to 2.0¢. Estimates, not results from past bookings. We count low on purpose: a premium cabin can price a point at 5¢ to 12¢, est.
    Verdict
    Clears the fee when even the floor beats $550 a month. Close call when only the top does. Under ~$25K a month on cards, not a fit yet.
