Field note 05 · What quietly disappears

The note,
in four points.

01Learn

Miles expire on different clocks

Some programs expire miles after 18 months without activity, some after 24 and some never. Each one is a trap if nobody is watching.

02Learn

Credits reset on staggered dates

One premium card can carry an airline credit, a hotel credit, a monthly ride credit and a twice-yearly retail credit, each on its own schedule.

03Learn

Free night certificates lapse

A hotel card’s free night is usually valid for about 12 months. It does not roll over, and you paid for it in the annual fee.

04Learn

A small purchase can save a balance

In many programs one small earning purchase resets the inactivity clock. A balance worth hundreds of dollars, est., can be saved in a minute.
