02 · Golden nuggets

What most contractors never hear
about their codes.

Every purchase carries a merchant code, and the code decides what a card pays for it.

The same 2x4 can code three ways

A lumber yard (5211), a home supply warehouse (5200) and a construction-materials distributor (5039) are separate categories. Rewards follow the code, not the item.

01

Bulk fuel at the yard isn't "gas"

Delivered bulk fuel typically codes as 5172 and pump fuel as 5542. A gas category may not cover your fuel-delivery invoice.

02

Rentals are their own category

Equipment rental (7394) and truck or trailer rental (7513) code separately from purchases.

03

Time the big materials buy to the draw

Illustration: a $40,000 materials order placed the day after your statement closes isn't due until the next statement's due date, which can be after the draw lands. Your dates will differ.
