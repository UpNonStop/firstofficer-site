The mistakes

What Landscaping Companies Get Wrong

None of this is obvious from a statement. It is what the desk sees on a landscaping company profile, line by line.

01

Putting nursery stock and materials on a debit card, or on a card that earns one point on it

Your biggest line, $38,000 a month, codes outside every bonus category on the cards that matter. A debit card earns nothing on it. The no-fee 2x card earns two points on the first $50,000 a year and one point after that. The flat-2x card with an annual fee earns two points on all of it. Neither is a bonus. Both beat the debit card by six figures of points a year.

The math

$456,000 a year. No-fee 2x card: 506,000 points. Flat-2x card with a fee: 912,000 points. Debit card: 0.

02

Leaving the lines that earn 3x and 4x on the same card as the invoice

The small lines are the ones that pay: fuel for trucks and equipment, $14,000 a month on this profile. On published terms, 4x holds on the first $150,000 a year of advertising, software, fuel and tolls combined, and 3x on the first $150,000 of shipping, travel, internet and phone combined, then 1x. This profile passes the pool: $18,000 a year of bonus-eligible spend runs at the next rate down. Past the pools, a second entity or a second issuer is a desk decision, not a promise.

The math

$14,000 a month on the bonus lines, routed: 672,000 points a year. On the invoice card at 2x: 336,000. On a debit card: 0.

03

Forcing rent, payroll and non-card vendors onto the card to chase points

Left off this profile: crew payroll. Rent and vendor bills can ride a bill-pay service at 2.9%; at this page's own defaults 2x is worth about 3.7 cents a dollar after the honest discount, so it clears by well under a cent. The desk decides those line by line.

The math

2 points × 3.0 cents × 85% redeemed × the honest discount = about 3.7 cents a dollar. Minus a 2.9% bill-pay fee = well under a cent. That is not a strategy; it is a rounding error with a fee attached.
