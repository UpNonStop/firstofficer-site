02 · Golden nuggets

What most landscaping companies never hear
about their codes.

Every purchase carries a merchant code, and the code decides what a card pays for it.

Nursery vs home center

A nursery typically codes as 5261 and a home center as 5200. The same flat of plants codes differently depending on where you buy it.

01

Equipment rental is its own category

Renting a skid steer or aerator typically codes as 7394, not as a purchase.

02

Bulk fuel isn't "gas"

Fuel delivered to the yard typically codes as 5172 and the pump as 5542. A gas category may not cover the delivery invoice.

03

Spring stacks the big buys

Plants, mulch and rentals often land in the same few weeks. An order placed the day after a statement closes isn't due until the next statement's due date. Your dates will differ.
