Field note 01 · What a point is worth

The note, in four points.

01Learn

The value ladder is hidden

Programs show the simple options first: statement credit, gift cards. The richer ones, transfers to airline and hotel partners, sit behind menus and partner lists.

02Learn

Cents per point is abstract

“1.6 cents a point” means little. “The ad spend paid for a hotel night” means a lot. It is the same math, est., so the desk always says it in dollars.

03Learn

Prices move by the day

Many programs price awards by demand. The same seat can cost very different points on different days, so a fixed rule of thumb breaks.

04Learn

The best value takes the most knowledge

The richest redemptions depend on three things at once: the transfer ratio, the award space and the timing. Nobody learns that by accident.
