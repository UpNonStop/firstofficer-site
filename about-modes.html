02 · The rules

Three rules we work by.

Rule one

Your yes, first.

Nothing moves without your approval. A recommendation is a recommendation until you say yes.

Rule two

No affiliate money.

Nobody pays us to recommend a card. The only person who pays us is you.

Rule three

Dollars, not points.

Every Friday, one number in dollars, checked against your statements.
