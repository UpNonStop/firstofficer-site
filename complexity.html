Field note 03 · Why it is this hard

The note,
in four points.

01Learn

Charts gave way to moving prices

Award charts once told you what an award cost. Many programs now price by demand, with no published chart to plan against.

02Learn

Bonus categories depend on codes

A bonus applies only when the merchant’s category code matches. A grocery pickup or a warehouse club may not code as a grocery store, and few people are told.

03Learn

Transfer ratios are not standard

Many bank points move 1:1 to airline partners. Some move at less, and some hotel points move at about 3:1. Each ratio can change.

04Learn

Terms change with little notice

Programs can change terms, earning rates and partners. What you learned last year may not hold this year.
