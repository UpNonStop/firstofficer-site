02 · Golden nuggets

What most trucking companies never hear
about their codes.

Every purchase carries a merchant code, and the code decides what a card pays for it.

Inside vs at the pump

The card networks define service stations (5541) and automated fuel dispensers (5542) as separate codes, so the same fill-up can code differently depending on where you pay.

01

Washes and parking are their own categories

A truck wash typically codes as 7542 and paid parking as 7523, not as fuel.
