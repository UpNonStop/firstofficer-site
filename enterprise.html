Straight answers

What procurement asks.
Answered here.

Can the desk move our money?

No. The desk cannot move money, open accounts or buy anything. It recommends; you approve; only then does it act. Connections are read-only.

Does a card issuer pay you?

No. Card issuers pay us $0, and there are no affiliate links, ever.

Are you certified?

First Officer holds no security certification today. If your review requires one, tell us before anything starts. More on Trust & Security.

Are you a financial advisor?

No. We are a rewards desk for business card spend, not a bank, lender, broker, or licensed financial, tax or legal advisor.

What does it cost?

It's priced for your setup: how many cardholders, entities and programs. Tell us about yours and we come back with one price.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Award space, transfer ratios and program rules change without notice. Every figure on this site is an estimate.
