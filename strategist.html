The Strategy Engine

Four Forces. One System.

Every strategy the desk builds for your business optimizes across four dimensions simultaneously. Weighted to your industry, your vendors, your goals.

01 EARN

Earn Smarter

Card routing rules matched to your actual vendor mix. Google Ads at 3x on the right ads card. SaaS subscriptions at 3x. Wholesale at 2x on a flat-rate everyday card (most bonus cards earn 1x on wholesale MCC codes like 5099). The desk recalculates monthly as your spend shifts.

02 BURN

Burn for Business Travel

Team conferences, client meetings, recruiting trips. The desk monitors award availability for your specific routes and dates. Transfer 200K points to Hyatt for 5 nights at a property costing $485 per night in cash. That is $2,425 your travel budget keeps.

03 RETURN

Measure ROI Per Card

Annual fee vs. points earned vs. redemption value. The desk calculates the net return on every card in your stack and tells you which ones are paying for themselves and which are dead weight. Monthly portfolio valuation included.

04 LEARN

Learn Your Industry

Every strategy session teaches your team something. Why routing Sysco through a flat-rate everyday card catches 2x when other bonus cards earn 1x on wholesale. Why splitting SaaS subscriptions across the right business cards captures 3x. The desk makes your finance team dangerous.

Business Intelligence

Finds What Your Accountant Missed

Vendor MCC codes that kill bonus rates. Employee card timing windows. Spend routing gaps worth thousands per year.

01
MCC Intel

Vendor MCC Mapping

Sysco and US Foods code as wholesale (MCC 5099/5199), earning 1x on most bonus cards. The desk maps every vendor to its actual MCC code and routes spend to the card that captures the highest rate.

02
5/24 Tracking

5/24 Window Management

Employee cards count against 5/24 for a major issuer applications. The desk tracks every team member and identifies windows for high-value signup bonuses in the 100K-point range.

03
Routing

Spend Category Splitting

Your $14K per month in Google Ads earns 3x on the right ads card (MCC 7311). Your $3K in SaaS earns 3x. But your $8K in miscellaneous earns 1x on a generic card. The desk splits spend to capture every multiplier.

04
Fee ROI

Annual Fee ROI Analysis

Is the $695 a premium travel card paying for itself through the airline credit, Uber credit, and lounge access for your 3 traveling team members? The desk calculates the net return on every card fee and recommends keeps, cancels, and downgrades.

05
Sequencing

Signup Bonus Sequencing

The 3x ads card first (100K-point signup). Then a premium business card (4x on top 2 auto-selected business categories). Then a no-fee everyday card (2x on everything). The order matters. The desk builds the 12-month application calendar.

06
Benchmarks

Industry Benchmarks

E-commerce: 2.2x to 2.8x blended. Restaurants: 1.8x to 2.2x. HVAC and contractors: 1.6x to 2.0x. The desk benchmarks your portfolio against your industry and closes the gap.
