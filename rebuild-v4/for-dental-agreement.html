FAQ

Questions practice owners ask first.

Is First Officer a credit card?

No. First Officer is an independent desk. We have no affiliate relationships with any issuer, so a card recommendation is only ever about your numbers.

Do I have to change the cards the practice uses?

Not by default. The desk starts with what you have. Any change is proposed with the math behind it, and nothing moves without your yes.

What does the estimate on this page include?

Points from spend times a blended earn rate, valued at about 3 cents a point, with 85% of points assumed used and a further discount for travel you would not have bought anyway. Our $550 monthly fee comes out before the net figure. It is an estimate, not a projection of your results.

How does booking a trip work?

You tell the desk what you want. Burn vets the requirements first: program rules, transfer terms and fees. Then the desk books it for you as an ad hoc service, and you confirm before anything is final.

Is this a one-time optimization?

No. You are an ongoing client. Programs change through the year, so the desk works your accounts every month, with white-glove service throughout.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any month. Month to month, no exit fee.
