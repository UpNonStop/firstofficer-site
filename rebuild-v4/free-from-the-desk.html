Contractors: 5 Golden Nuggets

The stuff I see on contractor statements that nobody mentions, like the 2x4 that codes three ways.

Visa, IRS1 Example
Grab it →
  1. The same 2x4 can code three ways. Lumber yard 5211, home supply warehouse 5200, materials distributor 5039. Rewards follow the code, not the item. Visa MDSM
  2. Paying a sub by card moves their 1099. The IRS says card payments go on Form 1099-K from the payment settlement entity, not on your 1099-NEC. Ask your preparer. IRS i1099mec
  3. Bulk fuel at the yard isn't "gas." Delivered bulk fuel is 5172. The pump is 5542. Visa MDSM
  4. Rentals are their own category. Equipment rental 7394, truck and trailer rental 7513. Visa MDSM
  5. Time the big materials buy to the draw. Bought the day after your statement closes, it isn't due until the next statement's due date. Your dates will differ. Example

HVAC: 4 Golden Nuggets

Refrigerant rules, parts-house codes and paying installers by card. Written for the shop, not the textbook.

Visa, IRS, EPA1 Example
Grab it →
  1. Refrigerant is sold to certified techs only. Under 40 CFR 82.154(c), the buyer must be certified or employ a certified technician and show proof. Who holds the card at the counter matters. EPA, 40 CFR 82.154
  2. Your parts house and your electrical supplier code differently. Plumbing and heating is 5074. Electrical parts is 5065. Visa MDSM
  3. Paying installers by card? Same 1099-K rule as above. IRS i1099mec
  4. Equipment buys bunch up before peak season. That's when timing your statement cycle matters most. Example

Script

5 questions for your supplier

Read them at the counter. Most reps have never been asked, and the answers are worth knowing.

Coding: Visa MDSM
Grab it →
  1. "What category does your card terminal run under? Can you check the merchant code on your processor statement?"
  2. "Do you add a surcharge for cards? Is it the same at every location and for every card type?"
  3. "Is there a different price for paying by bank transfer, or for net terms?"
  4. "Does your online ordering run through the same terminal as the counter?"
  5. "Can my receipts show the item lines, not just a total?"

The questions are ours. Merchant codes are defined in the Visa Merchant Data Standards Manual.

Checklist

Year-end card checklist

Seven things to look at before December 31, so January starts with no surprises.

Example
Grab it →
  1. Pull every card statement for the year into one folder.
  2. Check which suppliers you paid by card and which by transfer.
  3. List the contractors you paid by card. That's the 1099 question for your preparer.
  4. Note every annual fee and its renewal month.
  5. Write down each points balance and when it expires.
  6. Find the biggest purchases you know are coming next quarter.
  7. Check your statement close dates before those purchases.

An example checklist, not tax or financial advice.
