Field note 06 · Who the programs serve

The note,
in three points.

01Learn

You earn blind

Thirty swipes a month, and few owners know whether each one earned 1x, 3x or 4x. The same pump can code as fuel or as a convenience store.

02Learn

You redeem blind

A hotel night for 40,000 points can be a great or a poor use of them, depending on the date. The program does not say which.

03Learn

Prices go up

Programs revalue awards. A trip that costs 60,000 miles this year can cost more next year.
