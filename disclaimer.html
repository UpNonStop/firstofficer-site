What First Officer is, what it is not, and how to read the numbers on this site.

An educational tool

First Officer is an educational tool, not a licensed financial advisor. Nothing on this site is financial, tax or legal advice. Ask your accountant before acting on anything with a tax angle.

How the desk works

Your strategy is reviewed and fine-tuned by a person before it reaches you. Every redemption is booked by a person. Day-to-day answers are software-led, and a person steps in when needed.

Estimates are estimates

Point valuations are estimates based on common redemption benchmarks. Actual value depends on how and when you redeem. Calculator figures are estimates, not a projection of your results.

Award pricing

Award pricing and award space change constantly and we control neither. Not a guarantee of any outcome.

Illustrative examples

Every owner, business and conversation on this site is a fictional example, and every figure is an estimate, labelled est. Nothing here is a client story or a result.

Independence

UpNonStop has no affiliate relationships with any issuer. We are not paid by any card, bank or program for what we recommend.

Third-party trademarks

Bank, card issuer, airline, hotel and loyalty program names and logos appear on this site to identify the programs the desk tracks. They are trademarks of their owners, used for identification only. First Officer and UpNonStop are independent: not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of them, and no program has reviewed or approved anything here.

How the desk keeps current

First Officer keeps track of each program's published rules and keeps them current as they change. Nothing here is provided by, connected to or speaking for those programs. A person reviews every booking before it happens.

Who it is for

US businesses only. The monthly fee is $550, month to month. Award search is a separate $500 per trip, always.
