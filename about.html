01 · The problem

The programs have a team.
You have a Tuesday.

Meet Inés Vega. She runs Vega Family Dental in Tucson: two chairs, six people, about $11,000 a month in lab fees and supplies, est. Card programs, airlines and hotels each employ full-time people whose job is to protect their margin. Inés has patients at eight.

Nothing she did was careless. The instruments just kept moving while she was busy, and nobody was in the right seat to call it out.

What slipped past Inés last year, est.

02 · Why Ohad runs the desk

Four decades on planes.
Now on your side.

Ohad Sternberg has spent four decades on planes. Along the way he kept meeting smart, successful people who were losing to the fine print, and he came to believe something simple: most people need help to pull these programs apart.

So he put what he learned into software that does the watching, and kept a person on every move. His job now is the one a good first officer has. Ease the pain. Increase the gain. Let the captain fly.

03 · The way through

The desk does the watching.
You do the deciding.

You never see the machinery, and you shouldn't have to. Here is what it feels like from your side of the thread.

  1. 1All day, every day

    Software watches.

    Every program on your file: earn rates, partners, credits, price moves and fine print. When something changes that matters to you, it notices.

  2. 2Before anything reaches you

    A person checks.

    The math, the timing, the catch. If it isn't worth your thumb on a screen, you never hear about it.

  3. 3Your 10 seconds

    You say yes, or no.

    One plain message with the math attached. Nothing moves, and nothing is booked, until you approve it.
