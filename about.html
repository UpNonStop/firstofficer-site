The four jobs

Four jobs.
Every one is service.

Each job comes with a tradeoff the program won't put on the front page. Here they are, and what the desk does about each.

01 Earn

Which card for which spend.

The tradeoffA bonus category only pays if your purchase codes there. Merchants decide how it codes, not you.

What the desk doesMatches each big expense to the card that pays most for it, inside each card's caps.

02 Burn

Points into trips.

The tradeoffAward prices move, and sometimes cash is the better deal. Points sitting still tend to buy less over time.

What the desk doesPrices every trip in points against cash, vets the requirements with you, and books only after your yes.

03 Return

Friday, in dollars.

The tradeoffCredits and perks reset on a calendar, and annual fees renew whether you used them or not.

What the desk doesWatches every credit, perk and renewal date, and sends one number each Friday, checked against your statements.

04 Learn

Two minutes a week.

The tradeoffPrograms change their rules all the time. What worked last year may cost you this year.

What the desk doesOne short lesson a week, drawn from your own file, so you get sharper every month.

03 · Points or cash?

Is it a good redemption?
Test it in ten seconds.

Every redemption has a price per point. Work it out once and you'll never wonder again.

( cash price − taxes ) ÷ points = cents per point

1.6¢a point

Strong use, est. Better than a plain cash out.

  • Under 1¢: a cash out usually beats it
  • 1¢ to 1.6¢: a fair use
  • Over 1.6¢: a strong use, est.

Example prices are illustrative. A rule of thumb, not advice: the right use also depends on your plans, the cash fare's flexibility and what else the points could do.

04 · Who pays the adviser?

Ask who pays them.
Then ask us.

Lots of good people earn a referral fee when you open a card. That's fine, as long as you know. Advice follows the money, so here are four questions to ask anyone, us included.

  1. 1

    Who pays you?

    Listen for a straight answer, with a number in it.

    Our answerYou do. $550 a month, flat, month to month, no minimum term. No bank, airline or hotel pays us.

  2. 2

    Do you earn when I open a card?

    Listen for whether a link or a code is involved.

    Our answerNo. No affiliate links, no referral fees, no sponsored picks.

  3. 3

    Would you tell me to keep my card?

    Listen for a time they told someone to change nothing.

    Our answerYes, when the math says so. Our calculator never moves a line to a worse card.

  4. 4

    Will you show me the math?

    Listen for numbers you can check yourself.

    Our answerEvery move comes with its math, and nothing happens until you say yes.
