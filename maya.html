What we learned

Four things to act on.

Each insight comes with the change the desk will make. A person reviews them before anything changes for Maya.

Channel

Ask by text, before 9 a.m.

91% of her replies come by text, and Friday 7 to 9 a.m. is her busiest window. Email questions wait six hours.

→ Move all questions to text, Friday mornings
Motivation

The trip pulls her in, not the money.

She spends 4 minutes on Lisbon and under a minute on her spend chart. Value lands best when tied to the trip.

→ Show savings as "nights in Lisbon"
Device

Her plan lives on her phone.

75% of views are on a phone, mostly evenings. Desktop is Monday planning only.

→ Keep the plan page phone-first and short
Risk

Two open questions are stalling step 02.

Supplier coding has waited 9 days. Insurance timing affects $1,100 of the likely range.

→ A person on the desk calls if no reply by Oct 16
