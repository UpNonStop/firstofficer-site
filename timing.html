Field note 04 · When it has to happen

The note,
in three points.

01Learn

Transfer windows open and close

Programs sometimes add bonus points to transfers to one partner for a short window. Move the same points a day later and they go at the usual ratio.

02Learn

Award space is released on schedules

Many airlines release award space about 11 months ahead, and the best cabins tend to go first, est.

03Learn

Credits reset on different calendars

Calendar year, card year, quarterly, monthly. One card can carry four reset dates.
