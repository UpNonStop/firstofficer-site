The short version

Five commitments, in one place.

If you read nothing else on this page, read these.

01

Read-only

Every connection you grant is read-only. The desk can see transactions and balances. It cannot move money or make a purchase.

02

Your own sign-in

You connect through your provider's own sign-in. We never ask you for a password in the thread. If anyone claiming to be First Officer does, it is not us.

03

Your yes, first

Applications, transfers and bookings happen only after you say yes, in your own thread.

04

Not for sale

Your information is not sold or rented to advertisers, data brokers or card issuers. We take no affiliate commissions.

05

Yours to revoke

Revoke any connection from the provider's own settings, whenever you want, and ask us to delete your data.
