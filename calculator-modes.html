    Learn · the assumptions

    How the math works, plainly.

    No hidden multipliers. Everything the calculator uses is on this list, and every figure is an estimate.

    Earn rates
    Each line earns the rate shown, today and with your desk. "Your cards today" swaps your current rate for one flat card.
    Points used
    We count 85% of the points you earn. The rest stays out of every estimate.
    Point value
    1.0¢ a point at the low end (cash back) to 1.6¢ at the high end (a well-planned trip).

    About $25K a month on cards. Below that, the fee usually costs more than it finds, so we say it is not a fit yet.
