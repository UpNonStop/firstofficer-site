How we got this number ↓
Learn · how we got this number

Every step, in plain sight.
Basic
One number, one blended rate: about 1.2 points per $1 on your cards today and about 3.4 with your desk putting each line on its best card. That is the mix of a service business like the example Leo, so Basic at $35K matches him. Your own lines can earn more or less; Advanced uses the rates of each line.
Spend
Basic: your total a month. Advanced: your monthly card spend, by line, as typed. Examples are fictional owners.
Earn rates
Advanced: each line earns the rate shown per $1, today and with your desk. "Your cards today" swaps your current rate for one flat card.
Extra points
Points a month with your desk, minus points a month today.
Points counted
85% of the extra points. The rest stays out of every estimate.
Cents a point
Basic uses the mix. Advanced, by how you'll use the points: mixed 1.0¢–1.6¢, cash out 1.0¢, economy 1.0¢–1.4¢, top hotels 1.2¢–2.0¢, business class 1.5¢–2.0¢. Floor is a plain cash out. Estimates, not results from past bookings.
Found a month
Extra points counted × cents a point, at the middle of the range. The range uses the floor and the top.
Return
What your desk finds a month divided by the $550 a month fee.
Verdict
Clears the fee when even the floor beats $550 a month. Close call when only the top does. Under ~$25K a month on cards, not a fit yet.

Points prices move by date and program, so every figure here is an estimate. Your desk checks live prices with you and books only after you approve.
