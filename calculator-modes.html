How we got this number

The math, in plain words. Four steps, no magic.

The same four steps run on your Found Money Call, from your real statements. Every figure here is an estimate.

01Earn

Each line goes to its best card

Ads and software to the 4x card, internet and phone to 3x, the rest to 2x. That is your desk's points a month, next to what your cards earn today.

02Count

Only 85% of the new points count

Some points always go unused, so 15% stays out of every estimate. Your points today are not counted at all, only the extra.

03Value

Valued at how you will use them

A cash out is the floor, about 1¢ a point. Planned trips run higher. The answer is the middle of that range, and the range is shown.

04Fee

Then the $550 comes off

What your desk finds, against $550 a month, flat. Return is the one divided by the other. Under ~$25K a month on cards, it says not a fit yet.
