      Every step, in plain sight.
      Spend
      Your monthly card spend, by line, as typed. Examples are fictional owners.
      Earn rates
      Each line earns the rate shown, today and with your desk, per $1. "Your cards today" swaps your current rate for one flat card.
      Velocity
      Points a month: a year of earning divided by 12, posted monthly like a statement.
      Timeline
      The month your points with your desk first cover a trip, set against when today's cards would. Example points prices; no seats are held.
      Points counted
      85% of the extra points. The rest stays out of every estimate.
      Cents a point
      From the table above, by how you use them. Floor is a plain cash out. Estimates, not results from past bookings.
      Return
      Value of the extra points at the middle of the range, divided by the $6,600 a year fee. The range uses the floor and the top.
      Verdict
      Clears the fee when even the floor beats $6,600. Close call when only the top does. Under ~$25K a month on cards, not a fit yet.

      Points prices move by date and program, so every figure here is an estimate. Your desk checks live prices with you and books only after you approve.
