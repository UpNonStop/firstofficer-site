Pricing questions
How is the fee charged?
$550 a month, flat, the same for every business. The terms are in the Terms of Service.
Is the number above a promise?
No. Every figure is an estimate, labelled est. Your Found Money Call works it out from your real statements, and your Friday scorecard shows what actually happened.
Do you take money from card issuers?
No. No affiliate money from any bank, airline or hotel program.
Who makes the decisions?
You do. Every move waits for your yes, and your desk books only after you approve.
What if my spend is under ~$25K a month?
Then it most likely won't clear the fee, and the calculator says not a fit yet. Keep the card you have and come back when the spend grows.
How is the number worked out?
Your spend, at the rate it earns today and with your desk, 85% of the extra points counted, valued at how you will use them, then the $550 comes off. See every step.
