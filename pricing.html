Questions

Asked on every call.
Answered here.

The short answers. The Found Money Call covers the rest.

How is the fee charged?

Monthly and flat, the same for every business. Month to month, no minimum term. The terms are in the Terms of Service.

Does a card issuer pay you to suggest its card?

No. Card issuers pay us $0, and there are no affiliate links, ever. Your $550 is the same whatever card the desk suggests.

Can I stop any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like, from your billing page or in your WhatsApp thread. It ends with the month you've paid for, and there's no refund for part of a month.

What happens on the Found Money Call?

Twenty minutes with a person from the desk, on your real statements, and one number in dollars against $550. No card needed, no obligation.

What does award booking cost?

$500 per itinerary, flat, any party size, and only when you book. The desk vets the trip and books only after you approve.

Are card annual fees in the $550?

No. Annual fees belong to the card issuers. The desk only suggests a card when its points cover its fee.

Do I have to spend differently?

No. The fixes move a vendor to the right card. What you buy stays the same.

Who makes the decisions?

You do. Every move waits for your yes, and your desk books only after you approve.
