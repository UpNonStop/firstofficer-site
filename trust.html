The short version

Five commitments,
in one place.

If you read nothing else on this page, read these.

01

Read-only

Every connection you grant is read-only. The desk can see transactions and balances. It cannot move money or make a purchase.

02

Your own sign-in

You connect through your provider's own sign-in. We never ask you for a password in the thread. If anyone claiming to be First Officer does, it is not us.

03

Your yes, first

Applications, transfers and bookings happen only after you say yes, in your own thread.

04

Not for sale

Your information is not sold or rented to advertisers, data brokers or card issuers. We take no affiliate commissions.

05

Yours to revoke

Revoke any connection from the provider's own settings, whenever you want, and ask us to delete your data.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Are you certified?

First Officer holds no security certification today. If that is a requirement for your business, tell us before you sign up.

Are you a financial advisor?

No. We are a rewards desk for business card spend, not a bank, lender, broker, or licensed financial, tax or legal advisor.

Can the desk move my money?

No. The desk cannot move money, open accounts or buy anything. It recommends; you approve; only then does it act.

Can the desk find award space that do not exist?

No. Award space is set by each program. The desk looks for it and tells you plainly when it is not there.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Award space, transfer ratios and program rules change without notice. Every figure on this site is an estimate.
