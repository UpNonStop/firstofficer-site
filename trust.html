02 · The work

What the desk does.
What it never does.

The desk does

  • Reads every line of the spend you share.
  • Matches every bill to the card that earns the most on it.
  • Shows the card rule, the merchant code and the date checked behind every answer.
  • Checks every strategy with a person before it reaches you.
  • Counts what it found in dollars every Friday, checked against your statements.
  • Says no when the math does not clear the fee.
  • Leaves every connection yours to revoke, from the provider's own settings, whenever you want. Ask and we delete your data.

The desk never

  • Moves money, opens or closes accounts, redeems points or buys anything.
  • Asks you for a password. You connect through your provider's own sign-in. If anyone claiming to be First Officer asks for one, it is not us.
  • Sells or rents your information to advertisers, data brokers or card issuers.
  • Takes affiliate commissions.
  • Guarantees award space, transfer ratios or results.
  • Acts as a bank, lender, broker, or licensed financial, tax or legal advisor.

04 · What we don't claim

Straight answers
to the hard questions.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Are you certified?

Not today. First Officer holds no security certification. If your business requires one, raise it before you sign up.

Are you a financial advisor?

No. First Officer is a rewards service for business card spend. We are not a bank, lender or broker, and we are not licensed to give financial, tax or legal advice.

Can the desk move my money?

No. The desk cannot move money, open accounts or buy anything on its own. It recommends, you approve, and only then does it act.

Can the desk find award space that does not exist?

No. Award space is set by each program. The desk looks for it and tells you plainly when it is not there.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Award space, transfer ratios and program rules change without notice. Every figure on this site is an estimate.
