Questions

Plain answers. No fine print games.

What does First Officer cost?

The Found Money Call is free. If you keep the desk after it, First Officer is $550 a month, flat.

Is there a contract or an exit fee?

No. It is month to month, with no exit fee. Leave whenever you like.

Does the desk book trips for me?

Burn vets your requirements and the desk books them as an ad hoc service. Every decision stays yours.

Which programs are covered?

50+ programs across business cards, airlines and hotels. On the call we only talk about the ones your spend actually touches.

What happens on the Found Money Call?

Twenty minutes, free. We look at where your business spend goes on cards and show you what it could be collecting. Every dollar figure is labelled est. There is nothing to buy on the call.

Who is it for?

Business owners who put real spend on cards: suppliers, software, ads, fuel, travel. If the business runs on a card, the points are already piling up.

What happens to my data?

We use it to prepare your call and your estimate. Ask and we delete your data within 30 days.

Is this tax, legal or financial advice?

No. Everything on this page is general information, and every dollar figure is an estimate, not a promise.
