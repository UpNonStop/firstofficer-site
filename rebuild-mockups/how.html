The fixes

3 leaks. 3 fixes.

Almost every business we check is losing money in the same 3 places, and none of them require you to spend differently.

Leak 01 · Routing

Your spend codes wrong

Wholesale vendors, insurance, fuel and freight cap at 1x on most cards, and generic best-card advice does not know your vendors. We do, because we researched them. The fix is moving vendors, not changing what you buy.

+
Leak 02 · Burn

Your points get spent badly

Cashing out or booking in a card travel site prices your points at about a penny. Transferred into the right program at the right moment they have cleared several times that. The fix is where you spend them, not how many you have.

+
Leak 03 · Expiry

Your value quietly dies

Credits reset, certificates lapse, balances expire, and none of it announces itself. The fix is somebody watching the calendar that nobody at your company has time to watch.
