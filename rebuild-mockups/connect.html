What no way allows

The rules that do not move.

Money

We cannot move it

No payments, no transfers, no purchases. Every connection is read-only at the technical level, not as a policy we promise to follow.

+
Credentials

We never hold them

We do not see, collect or store passwords or card logins. Connections run through a regulated aggregator that keeps credentials away from us entirely.

Your points

They stay yours

Points remain in your loyalty accounts and you make the transfers. The exception is the separate $500 award search, when you want the desk hunting one specific trip.
