What First Officer Is

First Officer by UpNonStop™ is an AI-powered loyalty points advisory service delivered via WhatsApp. We help you understand your credit card rewards, optimize card usage, track expiring credits, and plan award travel, all through conversational intelligence.

First Officer is a software service. Most of the work, including recommendations, is done by software. A person from our team steps in when needed, and not on every message, so no individual message, recommendation or report should be assumed to have been reviewed by a person. Recommendations are for educational purposes only. First Officer is available to United States residents and businesses only.

First Officer is an educational advisory tool, not a licensed financial advisor. All recommendations are for informational purposes only. Your financial decisions are your own.

AI-Generated Recommendations

First Officer uses artificial intelligence (including models from Anthropic/Claude) to analyze your rewards portfolio and generate personalized recommendations. While we work hard to ensure accuracy, AI-generated information can contain errors. You should always verify recommendations before acting on them, especially before making transfers, bookings, or card applications.

UpNonStop provides information, research and advice. You hold your own points, in your own accounts, and you make your own bookings. UpNonStop is not a travel agency, tour operator, air carrier, ticket agent or seller of travel. We do not hold funds in trust, do not take custody of loyalty balances or account credentials, and do not book, ticket, hold or reserve travel.

Figures we publish are estimates based on stated assumptions and are not a projection of your results.

Award Search Service

The award search is available for $500 per itinerary, regardless of party size. The fee covers the search: availability across dates and routing options, the transfer path, the timing, and a fallback if the primary option disappears. You receive a written itinerary plan with the exact steps in order, and you execute the transfers and the booking yourself, in your own accounts. The fee is non-refundable once the search begins, which means once we start checking availability and pricing routes for your dates.

No Credential Storage

Our software and AI tools never store your loyalty program passwords, credit card login credentials, or banking credentials. Our automated systems never ask for or retain your login passwords.

Data Handling & Privacy

Your data is stored with row-level isolation in Supabase, meaning every client's data is completely separated at the infrastructure level. We handle access, correction and deletion requests to CCPA standards for every client, whatever their nationality or where they live. You can request full deletion of your data at any time by contacting us, and we complete it within 30 days.
