Golden nuggets

What most restaurants never hear about their codes.

🪙

Warehouse club vs grocery

A warehouse club is 5300 and a grocery store is 5411. The same case of product codes differently depending on where you buy.

🪙

Kitchen equipment is its own category

Commercial equipment dealers are 5046, separate from food purchases.

🪙

The tip credit

Certain food and beverage establishments can claim a credit for the employer's Social Security and Medicare taxes on certain employee tips, using Form 8846. Ask your preparer.

What we could not source yet: how broadline food distributors code. That varies by distributor and isn't published, so check your own statements.
