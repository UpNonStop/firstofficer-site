The money is already spent. The rewards it earned are real, and they sit uncollected: routed to the wrong card, expiring quietly, missing the window by a week. For example, a business running $80K a month can leave five figures a year at the gate. Individual results vary.

06 · The brain

Three data assets. One intelligence layer.

Every recommendation the desk makes is grounded in verified data: a live map of how cards actually behave, a knowledge base that admits only sourced facts, and your own profile kept in isolation.

108 cards · 13 issuers

The card map

Verified earning structures, merchant-code behavior, issuer rules, and transfer-partner routes across the full business card landscape. Re-verified weekly, because published charts drift and real-world coding drifts faster.

+
The library

A verified knowledge base

Client conversations surface candidate facts; each one is checked against a primary source, a real booking or a real statement, before it enters the corpus. Unverified claims never ship as advice.

Your profile

Your row, isolated

Your cards, vendors, balances, and goals, stored per client and never pooled. Anonymized patterns strengthen the map; your records and identity stay in your row.

And the asset compounds. A $34K-a-month medical practice gets a strong stack on day one, built from published rules and its own statements. The same question six months in returns a sharper answer, because more practices have been verified into the map in between.

The honest limits, stated plainly.

01
It does not move your money

Read-only on every connection. No transfers, no purchases, no applications without your explicit yes in the thread.
