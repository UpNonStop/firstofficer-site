What we access, what we never touch, and how you switch it off.

Plain answers, no jargon. First Officer reads your spending so it can find value you already earned. It never moves money, never holds your credentials, and never sells your information. Everything below is a commitment you can hold us to.

The short version

Five commitments, in one place.

If you read nothing else on this page, read these.

01
Read-only, always

Every connection you grant is read-only. First Officer can see transactions and balances. It cannot move money, open accounts, close accounts, redeem points, or make a purchase. There is no mode in which it can.

02
Our software never holds your credentials

You connect through your provider's own sign-in. Our software never sees, receives, or stores your passwords, and we never ask you to send them in the thread. If anyone claiming to be First Officer asks for a password, it is not us.

03
Nothing moves without your approval

Applications, transfers, and bookings happen only after you say yes in writing, in your own thread. A recommendation is a recommendation until you approve it.

04
We do not sell or rent your data

Your information is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or card issuers for marketing. We take no affiliate commissions, so there is no incentive to steer you anywhere.

05
You can revoke at any time

Revoke any connection from the provider's own settings whenever you want, without telling us first. Access ends immediately. You can also ask us to delete your data at any point, and we will.

Levels of access

You choose how much we see. Each level is optional.

Three levels, and you can stop at any of them or step back down at any time. More access means better advice, and less access still works.

LEVEL 1
Screenshots only

What it reads: only the numbers in an image you choose to send. What it never reads: anything else. Nothing is connected to any account. This is the same as texting a balance to a colleague.

LEVEL 2
Email connection

What it reads: travel, rewards, and expense messages, so bookings, transfers, and expiring credits can be tracked. What it never reads: the rest of your inbox, and it never sends mail as you. Granted through your email provider's official sign-in and revocable there.

LEVEL 3
Card and spend connection

What it reads: transactions and balances, so the desk can route spend to the right card. What it never reads or stores: your login, your full card number, and your account credentials. Read-only, revocable from your bank or from Plaid at any time.

Who else is involved

The companies that help run the desk, and why.

First Officer is a small operation built on established infrastructure. These are the services that may process your information, and the only reason each one does.

SUPABASE
Database and application hosting

Where your profile, spend context, and history are stored, on managed Postgres infrastructure in the United States, encrypted at rest and in transit.

META
WhatsApp Business Platform

Delivers the messages in your thread. Meta handles message transport under its own terms, the same as any WhatsApp Business conversation.

ANTHROPIC
Language model processing

Powers the software that reads your spend and drafts recommendations. Anthropic processes this data to return a response and does not use business API traffic to train its models.

PLAID
Read-only card and bank connections

Handles the connection itself when you choose Level 3, so your credentials go to Plaid and your bank, never to us.

STRIPE
Payments

Processes your $550 monthly fee. Your card details go to Stripe directly. We never see or store a full card number.

RESEND
Transactional email

Sends receipts and account email when message delivery is not the right channel.

How long we keep it

Your data has an expiry date too.

While you are a client, we keep what we need to do the work: your spend context, your portfolio, and your thread history, so the desk does not ask you the same question twice. When you cancel, connections are severed immediately and all of your personal data is deleted within 30 days. You can request deletion at any time, including while you are still a client, and you can ask for a copy of what we hold.

If something goes wrong

What we will do, and how fast.

If we confirm a security incident affecting your information, we will tell you directly in your thread within 72 hours of confirming it, describe what happened in plain language, say what data was involved, and tell you what to do next. We will not wait for a press cycle and we will not bury it in an email footer.

The integrity wall

Built into the foundation from day one.

01

No credential storage

Connections are read-only, always. Our software never sees, collects or stores passwords, card logins or banking credentials, and nothing moves without your written yes. The one exception is deliberate: when you engage the $500 award search, a person from our team steps in to work the search with you. We vet your requirements, and once you approve, a person on the desk books it in your own account, which is the only place your points ever sit. Our software is never in that loop.

02

Row level isolation

Every client's data lives in its own row, isolated at the database level. Patterns flow into the shared brain. Records never do.

03

No affiliate money

Nobody pays us to recommend a card. The only person who pays us is you, which is the only arrangement where our advice can be trusted.
