02 · What's live now

Before and after, on the live site.

Merged in PR #4 (privacy) and PR #5 (trust fixes), deployed and verified live on firstofficer.upnonstop.com. "Before" is the Oct 8 audit capture. "Live now" was captured tonight.

Homepage

"10 specialists" and "a human makes the call" are gone. The honest-no promise sits under the hero. The founder placeholder stays hidden until a real portrait exists.

Privacy

privacy.html now redirects to the one Privacy Policy (#do-not-sell), which says data is deleted within 30 days. Footer links point there.

Trust

"AI tools" became "software". "A human specialist" became "a person from our team steps in".

Welcome

Refund line and first-trip waiver removed. "Ohad closes it out by hand" became "we close it out for you".

Connect

Internal mockup strip removed, fixed dates became relative, and real card names became Card A, B and C.

Terms

"You are interacting with AI, not a human" now reads: software does most of the work, and a person steps in when needed, not on every message.

Pricing

Honest-no promise added. "10 specialists" became "software-led coverage across 50 programs, a person when needed".

Product

"10 specialists, a human desk" became "Coverage across 50 programs". "A human answers" became "A person when it matters".
